A workshop in the city of fine metalwork.

Kathmandu has been a center of Nepali metalwork for centuries. Grace Collection grew out of that tradition, one hand-finished piece at a time.

Grace Collection started as a small workshop with two benches and a handful of tools. The idea was simple: bring the same hand-finished quality found in Kathmandu's old metalworking lanes to jewelry people could wear every day, not just for one occasion.

Today the workshop is larger, but the process hasn't changed much. Every ring, chain, and clasp still passes through the hands of someone who has spent years learning to do it properly — sawing, soldering, setting, and polishing, mostly with the same tools the craft has always used.

We keep the collection small on purpose. It's easier to stand behind a hundred pieces you know well than a thousand you don't.

"I wanted people to wear something a person actually made — not a machine."
— Founder, Grace Collection

How we got here

  • 2014

    Grace Collection opens

    A small workshop opens in Kathmandu with two silversmiths and a single showcase.

  • 2017

    First bridal collection

    A dedicated line of wedding and engagement pieces launches, made to order.

  • 2020

    Orders across Nepal

    Grace Collection begins taking orders online, shipping to customers outside Kathmandu for the first time.

  • 2024

    Shipping internationally

    Select pieces become available to ship outside Nepal for the first time.

How a piece gets made

01

Sourcing

Metals and stones are sourced and checked against our standards before they reach the bench.

02

Design

Each piece is sketched and sized by hand before any metal is cut.

03

Handcraft

Our silversmiths shape, solder, and set every piece on the bench, not on a line.

04

Finishing

Polishing and a final inspection, so what leaves the workshop is ready to wear.
